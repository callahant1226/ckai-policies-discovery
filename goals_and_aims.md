# Hospital Policy + CKAI Prototype: Goals and Aims

## Purpose

The purpose of this prototype is to test the quality and presentation of responses with subject matter experts (SMEs) and users.

The system will present hospital institutional policy content alongside CKAI answers.

There will be two prototype variants:

- A physician prototype
- A nurse prototype

## Primary Goal

Assess whether the combined presentation of hospital institutional policy content and CKAI answers is useful, accurate and understandable for physicians and nurses.

## Key Risks To Address

### 1. Accurate Presentation Of Policy Content

Determine how to make sure policy content is presented accurately based on:

- The user’s use case
- The user’s workflow
- The user’s needs

### 2. User Trust

Determine how to make sure users trust the answer presented by the prototype.

### 3. Policy Content Extraction

Determine whether policy content can be extracted appropriately for use in the prototype, so that critical data ingestion requirements can be understood.

### 4. Conflicts Between Clinical Guidance And Policy Guidance

Determine how the prototype should treat situations where broader clinical guidance and hospital policy guidance conflict.

## Prototype Audience

The prototype will support:

- Physicians
- Nurses

The physician and nurse experiences will be treated separately because CKAI provides a physician variant and a nurse variant.
